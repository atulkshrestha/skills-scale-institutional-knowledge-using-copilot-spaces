# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Additional Personas and Specialist Roles

These roles make ownership for common project needs explicit. Depending on project size and risk, a role may be filled by a dedicated specialist, shared across team members, or brought in when needed; not every project requires a separate person for each role. The Project Manager remains responsible for coordinating delivery, and the Product Manager remains accountable for product outcomes.

### UX/UI Designer or Researcher

#### Role Summary
Designers and researchers understand user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences. They inform product decisions with research and translate requirements into validated designs.

#### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and share findings with the team
- Create and maintain journeys, interaction flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate usability and accessibility with users and agreed standards
- Capture design decisions and open questions for implementation

#### Collaboration
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs and research findings to priorities and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on research, design, and validation milestones
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and resolve implementation questions
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility expectations in validation
- Gather feedback from Stakeholders and communicate how it informs design decisions

### Engineering/Technical Lead or Architect

#### Role Summary
The technical lead guides engineering direction and helps ensure the solution is maintainable, feasible, and appropriately tested.

#### Responsibilities
- Propose or document architecture and technical decisions
- Identify engineering dependencies, estimates, technical risks, and mitigation options
- Support consistent implementation practices and technical reviews
- Guide Developers on system boundaries, integration points, and testability

#### Collaboration
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on sequencing, capacity, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Explain technical options and trade-offs to the Product Manager to inform scope and priority decisions
- Align Developers on implementation direction and review significant technical changes
- Partner with QA/Testing on testability, integration points, and technical acceptance
- Explain material technical decisions and risks to Stakeholders in accessible terms

### DevOps/SRE or Release Engineer

#### Role Summary
This role supports reliable operations and safe, observable deployments from release preparation through post-release verification.

#### Responsibilities
- Maintain or advise on deployment automation, environments, and operational readiness
- Define or contribute to monitoring, alerting, and observability needs
- Prepare deployment, verification, and rollback or mitigation procedures
- Support incident response and capture operational follow-up actions

#### Collaboration
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on release timing, readiness, risks, and incident communications
- Advise the Product Manager on operational impacts that affect rollout or product decisions
- Work with Developers on deployment configuration, instrumentation, and operational changes
- Align with QA/Testing on environment readiness, smoke tests, and release verification
- Provide Stakeholders with release status and relevant service-impact information

### Data/Analytics Lead

#### Role Summary
The data or analytics lead helps teams define, collect, validate, and interpret evidence about product and project outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Translate success measures into instrumentation and reporting requirements
- Advise on event definitions, data quality, privacy-aware collection, and metric interpretation
- Validate that dashboards or analyses represent agreed definitions
- Report outcome trends and limitations so the team can make informed decisions

#### Collaboration
- Define success metrics and measurement plans with the Product Manager
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on reporting cadence and delivery of agreed dashboards
- Work with Developers to implement and document instrumentation
- Partner with QA/Testing to verify event capture and data quality where applicable
- Explain findings, assumptions, and limitations to Stakeholders

### Security/Privacy Lead

#### Role Summary
The security or privacy lead advises on risks and controls so that security and privacy needs are considered throughout delivery.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security, privacy, and compliance risks and recommend mitigations
- Advise on security and privacy requirements, design reviews, and appropriate controls
- Help determine what security validation or specialist review is needed before release
- Support escalation through the applicable security or privacy incident process

#### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to include security and privacy needs in scope and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on risk ownership, mitigation tracking, escalation, and release readiness
- Advise Developers on secure design and implementation controls
- Partner with QA/Testing on validation of relevant controls and documented evidence
- Communicate material risks and required actions to Stakeholders through the agreed escalation path

### Customer Support/Operations Representative

#### Role Summary
This role brings customer-facing and operational support needs into planning and helps prepare teams to support a change after release.

#### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer issues and operational needs that may affect scope or acceptance
- Prepare or update support guidance, known-issue information, and escalation procedures
- Confirm support readiness before release and share post-release feedback
- Route customer-impacting issues through the agreed triage and incident processes

#### Collaboration
- Share customer impact and feedback with the Product Manager to inform priorities and follow-up
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on support readiness, release communications, and escalation
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to understand known issues, reproduction details, and expected behavior
- Provide Stakeholders with support impact, readiness status, and emerging themes
