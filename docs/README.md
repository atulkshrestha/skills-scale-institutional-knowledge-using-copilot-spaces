# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project lifecycle, with clear ownership and decisions informed by outcomes. During initiation, the team validates the problem, goals and success measures, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and resource needs before deciding whether to proceed. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, dependencies, and an initial QA approach.

During execution, teams deliver small increments and track work on a project board from Backlog and Ready through In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager defines outcomes and priorities; developers implement and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Communication keeps progress and decisions visible through regular standups, a weekly delivery sync, a weekly PM–Product Manager sync, and sprint or milestone demos and reviews. Standups may be daily or at another agreed cadence. Stakeholders receive regular, typically monthly or milestone-based updates, while blockers escalate from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.

Quality assurance spans development through release. PRs should be small where possible, link the relevant issue, and state acceptance criteria; CI runs automated tests and linting before review, with at least one approval or the team’s defined policy. Teams also use unit and applicable integration tests, security scans, manual QA when needed, and smoke tests for critical flows. Releases require acceptance criteria and CI/security checks to pass, a rollback or mitigation plan, and staging and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or important milestones capture learnings and track improvement actions.
